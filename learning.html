Common Questions

What You May Be Wondering

Do I need any prior knowledge to join a foundation course?+
None at all. Every foundation course is designed for complete beginners. All you need is genuine curiosity and a willingness to engage with the material.
Are the sessions live or recorded?+
All sessions are live — this is intentional. The transmission that happens in a live learning environment cannot be replicated by a recording. Sessions are recorded and shared with enrolled students for review, but attendance is strongly encouraged.
What platform are online sessions held on?+
Live sessions are held via Zoom. All you need is a stable internet connection and a quiet space. Session links are sent 24 hours before each class.
Can I pay in instalments?+
Yes — all programmes above ₹8,000 can be split into two or three instalments at no extra cost. The Sacred Arts Immersion year is available in monthly payment plans.
I am interested in coaching — how is it different from a reading?+
A reading reveals what is present — the patterns, timing, and energies at play. Coaching takes that insight and builds on it: helping you make real decisions, shift real habits, and navigate real change over time.
