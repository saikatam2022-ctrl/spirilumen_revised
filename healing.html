Common Questions

What You May Be Wondering

Do I need to believe in Reiki for it to work?+
No belief is required. Reiki works at the level of the body's own energy system, which operates independently of your conscious opinions about it. Many sceptics have been the most surprised by how much they felt.
What does a Reiki session actually feel like?+
Most people experience warmth, tingling, heaviness, or deep relaxation. Some feel emotional releases — tears, laughter, or a sense of lifting. Others fall asleep. All of these are signs the body is responding and processing.
How many sessions do I need?+
A single session can create noticeable shift. For deeper or more chronic concerns, three to five sessions over a few weeks tends to create the most lasting change. We always assess together rather than prescribing in advance.
Is distance Reiki as effective as in-person?+
Yes — and many clients actually prefer it. Energy is not bound by physical location. The main requirement is that you are resting comfortably and undisturbed for the duration of the session.
I have never meditated before. Where do I start?+
The Introductory Guided Meditation session is designed precisely for you. There is nothing you need to know or do beforehand — just arrive with an open mind and a willingness to sit still for a little while.
